Time tracking: log the time spent on a task or in a meeting (``log_time()``, ``start_timer()`` and ``stop_timer()`` on a task), read it back with ``time_log()`` and ``time_spent()``, and sum it per customer or budget line with ``calendaring.timetracking.summarize()``.  Entries are stored as ordinary calendar events, so any CalDAV client shows them and a calendar search finds a week's work.
