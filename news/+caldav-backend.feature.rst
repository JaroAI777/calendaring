A CalDAV backend, over the caldav library: ``pip install 'calendaring[caldav]'``, then ``Backend.connect("https://...", username=..., password=...)`` or a ``kind: caldav`` section in the configuration file.  It declares its capabilities from caldav's knowledge of the server, uses the server's sync-token for ``changes()`` where it is reliable, and passes the conformance suite against Xandikos and Radicale.
